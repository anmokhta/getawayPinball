# Project Brief: Silverball Social

## 1. Project Overview
**Silverball Social** is a premium, retro-modern pinball sanctuary located in the heart of the Pixel District. The project aims to blend the nostalgic tactile experience of classic pinball with a high-fidelity, "bleeding edge" social atmosphere.

## 2. Brand Identity
- **Name:** Silverball Social
- **Tagline:** The High Score Starts Here.
- **Personality:** High-octane, immersive, exclusive, and retro-modern.
- **Logo:** A neon-sign style graphic featuring a stylized pinball with motion trails in hot pink and electric blue.

## 3. Design System: "Neon Velocity"
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary:** Neon Lime (#d1ff00)
  - **Accent:** Hot Pink, Electric Blue
  - **Surface:** Deep Obsidian (#131315)
- **Typography:** *Anybody* — a bold, versatile sans-serif that captures the high-energy arcade aesthetic.
- **Visual Style:** Dark mode by default, utilizing high-contrast neon accents, glowing effects, and a grid-based "The Arsenal" machine gallery.

## 4. Sitemap & Core Features
### Home (Live)
- **Hero:** Immersive brand statement with primary CTAs (Book a Table, View Machines).
- **The Arsenal:** Featured pinball machines (Godzilla, Jurassic Park, Medieval Madness) with difficulty ratings and stats.
- **The Circuit:** Event calendar for tournaments like "Friday Night Flip-Off" and IFPA sanctioned matches.
- **Elite Access:** Membership tiers offering unlimited play, secret menus, and exclusive merch.
- **Find the Vibe:** Location mapping and venue contact details.

### Planned Screens
- **Machine Catalog:** Full inventory of 40+ world-class tables with filtering by manufacturer (Stern, Williams, Bally).
- **Tournament Leaderboards:** Live ranking system for local players.
- **Membership Portal:** User account management and exclusive content access.
- **Booking Flow:** Reservation system for tables and private events.

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Device Support:** Desktop-first (High-Fidelity), with mobile-responsive layouts.
- **Interactivity:** Scroll-triggered neon glows, hover states for machine cards, and real-time event integration.
- **Architecture:** Persistent app shell for seamless navigation across the arcade experience.
